Ask Our Geriatric Nurse & Physician Panel

Real questions submitted by family caregivers, answered by our clinical directors.

Question from Mark S. (Austin, TX):

"How do I gently bring up in-home care without offending my fiercely independent father?"

Dr. Sarah Jenkins advises: "Never frame it as a loss of independence. Frame it as support that enables him to remain in his home longer. Introduce the caregiver as a 'personal assistant' for heavy lifting, meal prep, or driving, rather than a nurse."

Dr. Sarah Jenkins, MD Geriatrics
Question from Linda K. (Chicago, IL):

"What vital signs should I be monitoring daily for a parent with congestive heart failure?"

Nurse David Mitchell advises: "Daily weight is the most crucial vital sign for CHF. A sudden gain of 2–3 lbs overnight or 5 lbs in a week signals fluid retention before shortness of breath appears. Always weigh first thing in the morning."

David Mitchell, BSN, RN-BC
