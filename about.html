Our Heart & Soul

Rooted in Dignity, Driven by Compassion

Since 2011, EverGrace has stood as a beacon of warmth and medical rigor, supporting seniors in living rich, autonomous lives while giving families true peace of mind.

Founded in 2011

Born From a Personal Family Journey

When our founder, Clara Vance, faced the difficult choice of finding nursing care for her grandmother who had early-stage dementia, she observed a painful gap in the healthcare market: cold clinical facilities on one end, and untrained unverified sitters on the other.

Clara assembled a core team of geriatric nurse practitioners, social workers, and physical therapists to construct EverGrace — a home-centered care agency where seniors are celebrated as beloved family elders, rather than patient file numbers.

"To care for those who once cared for us is one of the highest honors in human life."

— Clara Vance, Founder & President
Guiding Principles

Our Mission, Vision & Core Values

Every decision we make, from caregiver onboarding to daily medication routines, is anchored in our core values.

Uncompromised Dignity

We uphold the autonomy, privacy, and personal preferences of every senior in our care, respecting their lifelong wisdom.

Active Empathy

We listen patiently, hold hands during moments of anxiety, and celebrate the small everyday joys and milestones.

Clinical Rigor

Registered Nurse supervision, verified vital records, rigorous fall protocols, and strict medication compliance.

Family Transparency

Clear, honest communication with adult children and guardians via our encrypted live portal and weekly RN syncs.

Uncompromising Safety

The Industry's Strictest 7-Step Caregiver Vetting

We know you are welcoming a caregiver into the most sacred place: your parent's home. We accept fewer than 1 in 12 applicants to ensure uncompromising trustworthiness and clinical proficiency.

  • 01.
    State & FBI Criminal Fingerprint Background Checks across all 50 states and motor vehicle records.
  • 02.
    Full Medical Credential & License Verification with state boards of nursing (CNA, HHA, LPN, RN).
  • 03.
    Hands-On Clinical Skills Assessment in transfer techniques, vitals, infection control, and fall prevention.
  • 04.
    Alzheimer's & Dementia Behavioral Certification including non-verbal calming and Montessori engagement.
  • 05.
    Comprehensive Drug Screening & Health Physicals repeated annually.
