Predictable Routine

A Day in the Life of an EverGrace Senior

Routine brings comfort, dignity, and cognitive stability. Here is how our caregivers nurture health and happiness throughout a typical day:

08:00 AM
Morning Wake-Up, Hygiene & Vitals

Assisted bathing, oral care, medication administration, and recording of blood pressure and pulse.

12:00 PM
Nutritious Lunch & Cognitive Enrichment

Freshly prepared diabetic/heart-healthy meals followed by crossword puzzles, memories conversation, or light walking.

03:30 PM
Physical Therapy & Outdoor Garden Time

Seated mobility exercises, hydration check, and enjoying natural sunlight in the fresh air.

07:30 PM
Evening Wind-Down & Night Safety Protocol

Comfortable dressing, evening medication, night-light checks, and fall-safe bedroom setup.
